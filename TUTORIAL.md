# Tutorial
Welcome to Critic Tutorial. Today, I'm going to show you how to set up custom instructions in ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, etc.

## ChatGPT
### On PC/web app:
1. Go to chatgpt.com
2. Make sure you're logged in your account
3. Click on your profile (at the bottom left)
4. Go to "Settings"
5. Click on "Personalization"
6. Find "Custom instruction"
7. Type your custom instruction (e.g. Be friendly and helpful.)
8. Done!

### On iPhone/Android app:
1. Go to the "ChatGPT" app
2. Click on the settings icon (if logged in)
3. Go to "Personalization"
4. Type your custom instruction (e.g. Be friendly and helpful.)
5. Done!

## Gemini
### On PC/web app:
1. Go to gemini.google.com
2. Make sure you're logged in your account (Google, Yahoo!, Mail.ru, or Yandex)
3. Click on the settings icon
4. Click on "Gem"
5. Click on "New Gem"
6. Type your prompt (e.g. Help me build a workflow that connects to HTML5 and JavaScript)
7. Wait for Opal to create your app
8. Follow the steps
9. Done!

### Without Gem:
1. Go to gemini.google.com
2. Make sure you're logged in your account (Google, Yahoo!, Mail.ru, or Yandex)
3. Click on the settings icon
4. Go to "Personalization settings"
5. Click on "Saved info"
6. Click on the plus icon
7. Type your custom instruction (e.g. Be friendly and helpful.)
8. Make sure you're on a new chat when saving settings
9. Done!

### On iPhone/Android app:
1. Go to the Gemini app or gemini.google.com/app
2. Click on the settings icon
3. Tap on "Personalization settings"
4. If nothing was redirected to gemini.google.com/personalization-settings, go to [gemini.google.com/app](gemini.google.com/app)
5. Click on the settings icon
6. Tap on "Personalization settings"
7. If didnt work, click on [the link](https://gemini.google.com/saved-info)
8. Type your custom instruction (e.g. Be friendly and helpful.)
9. Make sure you're on a new chat when saving settings
10. Done!
